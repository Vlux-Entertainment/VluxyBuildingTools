Geometry

Part specs and the selection helpers the tools share. A spec describes a Part to build; it becomes a real Part only when Materialize runs inside an undo recording.

Properties​

FIDELITY​

</>
Geometry.FIDELITY: Enum.CollisionFidelity

The collision every cut and union is made with. The default only roughly follows the shape, so a hole in a thin wall stayed solid; this follows it closely, so holes can be walked through.

UnionArchHalves​

</>
Geometry.UnionArchHalves(
model: Model--

the model an arch was built into

) → string?--

why it could not join the pieces, or nil when it did

Joins the curved pieces of a built arch into two crescents, a left half and a right half. The posts that stand below the curve are left as they are. The joined pieces are taken out of the model with their Parent set to nil, not destroyed, so undo can bring them back.

UnionSides​

</>
Geometry.UnionSides(
model: Model,
pieceName: string,--

the name shared by the curved pieces

prefix: string--

the halves are named prefix .. "_Left" and prefix .. "_Right"

) → string?--

why it could not join them, or nil when it did

Joins the curved pieces of a built arch into a left and a right half, for arches that have no posts to split by. The two pieces farthest apart sideways give the centre and the span, and the joined pieces leave the model with their Parent set to nil so undo can bring them back.
