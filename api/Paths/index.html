Paths

Walls and corridors drawn along a path, the arched corridor, and how one segment of a chain bends into the next.

Part of the builders: each takes the clicked points and the settings and returns Part specs. Nothing here changes the place; the specs are previewed, then built by Geometry.

A corridor with a rounded ceiling: a floor, two straight side walls, and a half-circle vault above them that spans the clear width. The panel height is the total height at the centre, arch included, so the side walls are that height minus the radius of the vault.

Bend​

</>
Paths.Bend(
joint: Vector3,--

where the two segments meet, at the base level

prevDir: Vector3,--

the direction of the earlier segment

dir: Vector3,--

the direction of the new segment

prevStrips: {Strip},--

the strips of the earlier segment

strips: {Strip}--

the strips of the new segment

) → Bend--

finish and start are keyed by strip key, in studs along each segment's direction

Closes the corner where a wall or corridor continues another, with an exact miter and no overlap. Each strip (a wall, a floor) is cut along the line that bisects the bend: the strip keeps a plain rectangle up to the shorter end of that cut, and a small triangular prism fills the rest. A right angle needs no triangle: the earlier strip runs on to cover the corner square. The earlier segment's rectangles move by finish and the new segment's by start; the triangles go in ending and beginning.
