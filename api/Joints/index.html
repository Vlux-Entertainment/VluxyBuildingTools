Joints

Joins rooms, corridors and arched corridors where they meet: the cursor snaps to the walls of the ones already built, and each new one cuts openings through the walls it passes through, and has its own walls cut by the others. The structures are never merged into one union; only the walls get holes, made with subtraction.

Remembers a structure's cutters on the model, relative to its pivot, so they follow the structure when it is moved, turned or flipped.
