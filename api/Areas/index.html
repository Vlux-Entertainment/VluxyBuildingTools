Areas

Footprint shapes drawn from two corners: floors, foundations, porches, roofs, rooms and blocks, and the single pillar.

Part of the builders: each takes the clicked points and the settings and returns Part specs. Nothing here changes the place; the specs are previewed, then built by Geometry.

Functions​

Floor​

</>
Areas.Floor(
settings: Settings,
a: Vector3,--

one corner, at the height of the top of the floor

b: Vector3--

the opposite corner

) → {Spec}--

one flat slab as thick as the panel's floor thickness

Porch​

</>
Areas.Porch(
settings: Settings,
a: Vector3,--

one corner

b: Vector3--

the opposite corner

) → {Spec}--

a floor, four corner posts and, when switched on, a railing between the posts

Roof​

</>
Areas.Roof(
settings: Settings,
a: Vector3,--

one corner, at the height of the top of the walls

b: Vector3--

the opposite corner

) → {Spec}

Builds a roof over the rectangle between two corners, bigger by the overhang on every side. Gable and shed roofs are solid; hip and pyramid roofs are made of sheets; flat is a single slab.

Room​

</>
Areas.Room(
settings: Settings,
a: Vector3,--

one corner

b: Vector3--

the opposite corner

) → {Spec}--

floor, four walls and an optional ceiling; empty when the room is too small

Block​

</>
Areas.Block(
settings: Settings,
a: Vector3,
b: Vector3,
lock: AxisLock.Lock?--

the lock in force, if any

) → {Spec}

A box between two corners. With an axis lock the box follows the drag: the locked axis sets that side, and a Y lock sets the height instead of the panel value. Sides the drag does not cover are one snap step wide.

Pillar​

</>
Areas.Pillar(
settings: Settings,
base: Vector3--

the point the pillar stands on

) → {Spec}--

one square pillar as tall as the panel height
