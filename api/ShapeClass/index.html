ShapeClass

Sorts a part's size into the kind of shape it is, for the Unity color theme: blocks are orange, thin planes and pipes are grey.

Types​

Kind​

</>
type Kind = "Block" | "Plane" | "Pipe"

Plane: one side is much thinner than the others, such as a wall or a floor. Pipe: one side is much longer than the others, such as a beam, rail or post. Block: anything else, a cube or a chunky rectangular box.

Properties​

THIN​

</>
ShapeClass.THIN: number

How much smaller a side must be than the next to count as thin: a quarter.

Functions​

Of​

</>
ShapeClass.Of(
x: number,
y: number,
z: number
) → Kind--

the kind of shape a box of that size is; a box with a side of zero or less counts as a plane

LiesFlat​
