Host

Runs the one modal action that may be active (see Modal/Types), and the armed one-key actions: after Ctrl+M (flip) or O (rotate 90), the next X, Y or Z says which axis.

Types​

Armed​

</>
type Armed = "Flip" | "Rotate90"

A one-key action waiting for its axis.
