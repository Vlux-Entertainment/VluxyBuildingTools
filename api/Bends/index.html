Bends

Joins one segment of a chain to the one before it. When a wall, corridor or vent turns, each layer of the old segment (left wall, floor, right wall and so on) is stretched or shortened so it meets the matching layer of the new one, and corner filler pieces are added.

Bends the previous segment to meet the one just placed, and remembers the new one as the segment the next will join. Runs inside the build's undo step.
