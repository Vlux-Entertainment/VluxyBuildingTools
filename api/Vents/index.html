Vents

Square ducts, the cutters that open walls for them, and the end pieces: grate, cap, fan, peek cover and the hole marker.

Part of the builders: each takes the clicked points and the settings and returns Part specs. Nothing here changes the place; the specs are previewed, then built by Geometry.

Functions​

VentStrips​

</>
Vents.VentStrips(settings: Settings) → {Strip}--

the floor, ceiling and two side sheets of a vent, measured from its bottom

VentRun​

</>
Vents.VentRun(
settings: Settings,
a: Vector3,
b: Vector3,
joinDir: Vector3?--

the direction of the vent this one continues

) → {Spec}

A level vent from a to b. Both points are at the bottom of the duct. When it continues another vent its corner is closed the same way a corridor's is.

VentDuct​

</>
Vents.VentDuct(
settings: Settings,
a: Vector3,
b: Vector3
) → {Spec}

A straight vent in any direction, for slopes and vertical shafts. The points are on the middle line of the duct.

VentCutters​

</>
Vents.VentCutters(
settings: Settings,
a: Vector3,--

one end, on the middle line

b: Vector3--

the other end

) → {Cutter}

The shapes that are cut out of whatever a vent passes through: a box the size of the duct along its length, and the opening at each end, reaching a little into the wall behind it.

VentJoinCutters​

</>
Vents.VentJoinCutters(
settings: Settings,
a: Vector3,
b: Vector3
) → {Cutter}

The openings that let a vent join the side of another vent: a box at each end that starts just inside the vent and reaches out past its end, far enough to cut through the wall it ends in.

VentCover​

</>
Vents.VentCover(
settings: Settings,
center: Vector3,--

the middle of the grate, already lifted off the surface

normal: Vector3--

the way the surface faces

) → {Spec}

A grate: a thin frame with slats, standing on a surface.

VentCap​

</>
Vents.VentCap(
settings: Settings,
center: Vector3,--

the middle of the opening, lifted a little off the surface

normal: Vector3--

the way the opening faces

) → {Spec}

A plain square plate that closes the end of a vent.

VentFan​

</>
Vents.VentFan(
settings: Settings,
center: Vector3,

) → {Spec}

A frame with four fan blades set at an angle, closing the end of a vent.

VentPeek​

</>
Vents.VentPeek(
settings: Settings,
center: Vector3,
normal: Vector3
) → {Spec}

A small rectangular louvred cover, for looking out of a vent through a wall.

</>

the side of the hole

A square outline lying on a surface, showing where a vent hole will be cut.
