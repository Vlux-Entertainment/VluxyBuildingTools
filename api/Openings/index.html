Openings

Openings cut into a wall (doorways, windows, archways, any outlined shape) and the face extension of a block.

Part of the builders: each takes the clicked points and the settings and returns Part specs. Nothing here changes the place; the specs are previewed, then built by Geometry.

Cuts a door, window or arched doorway into the plain wall under the cursor, by rebuilding the wall as separate Parts around the hole.

CutShape​

</>
Openings.CutShape(
corners: {Vector3},--

the polygon's corners, on the face of the wall

triangles: {{number}},--

triples of indexes into corners

depth: number,--

how far the cut reaches, centred on the corners moved by shift

shift: Vector3--

where the corners move to, into the middle of the wall

) → {Spec}

The tool that cuts a polygon out of a wall: each triangle of the polygon is extruded through the wall as a pair of wedges, to be subtracted from it.
