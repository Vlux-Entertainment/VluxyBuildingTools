# Introduction

**VluxyBuildingTools** is a Roblox Studio plugin for building maps fast. You click a start and an end,
and it builds a wall, a room, a corridor or a flight of stairs. The keys work like Blender's.

It opens as a panel docked in Studio, with these tabs:

| Tab | What you do there |
|---|---|
| **Build** | Pick a tool and set its sizes. |
| **Props** | Pick a ready-made prop to place. |
| **Arrange** | Flip the selection, or make a row of copies with the Array. |
| **Display** | Color parts by theme, show a grid on surfaces, hide parts. |
| **Hotkeys** | Every hotkey in one list. |
| **Settings** | Snapping, standard sizes, the panel's look, sounds and reset. |

## A short tour

- **Draw, don't drag.** Walls, floors, rooms, corridors, stairs, ramps, arches, roofs, porches,
  fences, railings and vent ducts. A ghost preview follows the mouse, and you can type an exact length.
- **Cut openings.** Doorways (with a door and frame), windows, round and triangle windows, archways,
  vent holes, and any shape you outline.
- **Blender hotkeys.** G grabs, R rotates and C scales the selection. X, Y and Z lock an axis.
  T extrudes a face. Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+7 pick a tool.
- **One undo step per build.** Ctrl+Z takes back the whole thing.
- **Safe by design.** The plugin only edits parts inside one working folder that you choose.
- **Made for teams.** Themes and the grid are shared through the folder in Team Create. Hidden parts,
  the camera light and the sounds stay on your own screen.
