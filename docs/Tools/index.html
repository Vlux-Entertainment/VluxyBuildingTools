Tools

The Build tab shows a grid of tool tiles. Most tiles hold several tools; pick one from the buttons under the grid, or press Alt+1 to Alt+0. A tile starts the tool you used last.

Under the buttons are the options for the tool that is on. Some tools have more under Advanced options. Hover any option to see what it does. Right-click a number to put it back to its default.

Every tool needs a working folder, and every build is one undo step.

Drawing basics​

These work in every drawing tool:

Do thisTo
ClickPlace a point, or build.
Hold ShiftSnap to the fine grid.
X, Y, ZLock to an axis. Alt with it locks to the plane across that axis.
Type 0–9 and .Set an exact length.
[ and ]Lower or raise the height by one grid step.
EnterStart a new chain, or finish.
Esc or right-clickDrop the current point; again to stop the tool.

Points also snap to the corners and edges of nearby parts (see Settings).

Basic (Ctrl+1)​

Walls, floors, blocks, pillars and arches.

ToolHow to use it
WallsClick points; Enter starts a new chain. Y drags the height.
Pillared WallLike Walls, with a pillar at each end.
FloorClick two corners.
BlockClick two corners, then press Y to drag it up.
PillarClick to place a square pillar.
ArchClick the two inner edges at ground level.

Options: Height, Thickness, Pillar width, the floor's Transparency, and for the arch Clear height and Frame depth. Advanced: Floor / ceiling thickness and Arch smoothness.

Stairs (Ctrl+2)​

ToolHow to use it
StairsClick the bottom step, then the end of the run.
RampClick the bottom, then the top.

Options: Clear width, Total stair rise or Ramp rise. Advanced: Approx. step depth and Top landing depth.

Additive (Ctrl+3)​

Cut openings into walls, or grow blocks.

ToolHow to use it
DoorwayClick a plain wall.
WindowClick a plain wall.
Circle WindowClick a plain wall.
Triangle WindowClick a plain wall.
ArchwayClick a plain wall.
Cut ShapeClick corners on a wall; click the first corner again or press Enter to cut.
Vent HoleClick a wall, floor or vent.
Face ExtendSelect a block, then drag a green handle outward. The new block gets handles too.

Openings are centred on the cursor. With Set cut depth with mouse on (the default), the first click fixes the spot and the mouse sets how deep the cut goes; past the far side it cuts all the way through. Turn it off to cut through in one click.

Options:

  • Doorway: width and height, Door frame, Door (a see-through panel), Door transparency and Door CanCollide. Advanced: frame width and depth, door thickness.
  • Windows: width, height and Bottom above floor.
  • Archway: width and height. Advanced: Arch smoothness.
  • Vent Hole: Vent inside size, and Inside or Outside: cut the vent's inside size, or its outside size so a vent fits through. Advanced: Vent sheet thickness.

Structures (Ctrl+4)​

Rooms, corridors, foundations, porches and roofs.

ToolHow to use it
RoomClick two corners. A floor and four walls.
CorridorClick a start and an end; keep clicking to chain.
Arched CorridorLike Corridor, with an arched ceiling.
FoundationClick two corners. A thick slab under a building.
PorchClick two corners. A floor with posts and a railing.
RoofClick two corners over the top of the walls.

Options:

  • Room: Open, Shared or Separate for rooms that touch (no wall between them, one shared wall, or both walls).
  • Room and Corridor: Ceiling adds a ceiling.
  • Rooms and corridors: Join rooms and corridors cuts openings where they meet.
  • Height, Thickness and Clear width as they apply.
  • Foundation: Slab height and Margin around.
  • Porch: Post spacing, Railing on or off, Railing height.
  • Roof: Gable, Hip, Shed, Pyramid or Flat, Roof rise and Overhang. Advanced: Roof sheet thickness.

Vents (Ctrl+5)​

Square ducts and their end pieces.

ToolHow to use it
VentClick points; Enter starts a new chain. Turns at right angles.
Slope VentClick the start and end, then move up or down and click.
Shaft VentClick a surface, then move up or down and click.
Vent CoverClick a vent opening or any surface. Places a grate.
End CapClick a vent opening or any surface. Closes it with a plate.
Fan EndClick a vent opening. Closes it with a fan.
Peek CoverClick a wall or vent opening. A small cover to look out of.

Options: Vent inside size (the clear space inside; the sheets go outside it). Advanced: Vent sheet thickness.

Railings (Ctrl+6)​

ToolHow to use it
RailingClick points; Enter starts a new chain. Follows stairs.
Half WallClick points. A low solid wall.
FenceClick points. Posts and rails.

Options: Railing height, Bar spacing, Fence height and Post spacing.

Brush (Ctrl+7)​

Paints copies of objects onto surfaces.

  1. Select an object and click Add selected to brushes. (Or Ctrl+click an object with the brush on.)
  2. Click or drag over surfaces. A drag is one undo step.

Each brush in the list can be switched on or off, or removed with ✕. The brush picks one of the brushes that are on at random for every copy. With no brushes, it paints the selection.

Options: Copy rotation, Brush radius, Brush spacing, Random rotation. Advanced: Smallest size and Biggest size, Surface gap, Tilt copy to surface.

Props​

The Props tab holds ready-made props, in sections (All, Bathroom, Bedroom, Kitchen and more). Pick one, then click a floor or wall. Floor props stand on the surface; wall props hang flat on it. Press R to turn the prop 90°. Ctrl+scroll steps through the sections.

Options: Rotation, Prop size, Randomize prop rotation with how far (in degrees), and Collapse props after picking one.

Measure (Ctrl+0)​

Click two points to see the distance between them. The label stays until Esc or the next measurement.

Extrude (T)​

Point at a face of a plain block and press T. The mouse pulls the face out into a new block; type a number for an exact length. Click or press Enter to build it, Esc to cancel. With one block selected and nothing under the cursor, T uses the face that looks most toward the camera.

Grab, rotate and scale (G, R, C)​

Select parts, then press:

KeyAction
GGrab: move the selection.
RRotate. The arrow keys turn it 90°.
CScale.
VDuplicate, then grab the copy.

The selection follows the mouse. X, Y or Z locks an axis, Alt with it a plane, and a typed number sets an exact value. Click or Enter confirms; Esc puts everything back.

Two quick one-key actions:

  • Ctrl+M, then X, Y or Z: mirror the selection on that axis.
  • O, then X, Y or Z: turn it 90°. Hold Alt for -90°.
